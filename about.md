# About Me



I am **Ziad Salah**, also known as **ziad686**. I am a cybersecurity researcher and CTF player with **0xL4ugh**, focused on **Pwn**, vulnerability research, and practical security work.



I write about cybersecurity, CTFs, exploitation, privacy, and the lessons I learn while researching real-world software and systems.



## Vulnerability research



I have been credited as the finder of two published CVEs:



- [CVE-2026-56876](https://www.cve.org/CVERecord?id=CVE-2026-56876) — an `extract-zip` unvalidated symlink path-traversal vulnerability.
- [CVE-2026-67201](https://opencve.alliance.unm.edu/cve/CVE-2026-67201) — an SSRF bypass in V caused by a parser differential between `net.urllib` and `net.http`.
You can find my code, research, proof-of-concept work, and CTF projects on [GitHub](https://github.com/ziad626).



This blog is where I document what I learn, share technical write-ups, and build a public record of my security research.
